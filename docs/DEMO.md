# A five-minute walkthrough

Use sample data only. The app opens in Student view for Firm 1; the Preview team selector can show any of the five sample firms.

1. Open Decisions. Show the four directly accessible parts. Clear a required field and choose Save & continue to show validation. Restore it, save, then review and submit Firm 1.
2. Switch Preview team to Firm 5 and submit its existing sample strategy. Firms 2, 3, and 4 are already submitted.
3. Switch Demo experience to Professor view. Review all submissions and inspect one strategy.
4. Open Files & import, lock the round, and download the five generated RFI files as a ZIP. Show a fixed-width preview.
5. Explain that the professor runs the existing FORTRAN engine manually. Do not claim that the app runs it.
6. Choose Try supplied Q3 file. Preview all five parsed reports, import, and preview the results. Release the results when ready.
7. Switch to Student view, open Results, and select Q3. Show sales, net income, net margin, food cost ratio, and menu performance.
8. Open Team Studio. Explore the data journey, design rationale, current-vs-proposed database model, and the five branch responsibilities. Edit a project note or architecture rationale and save it; reload to show persistence.
9. Use the question-mark button for the guided design and architecture walkthrough. Toggle light/dark mode and resize to a phone.

Team Studio edits and student decisions are durable shared records. The local browser preview database and the deployed database are separate. Changing the role selector previews a screen; it does not authenticate a professor or enforce student team permissions.
